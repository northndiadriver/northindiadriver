SUGGESTED JOURNEYS

Realistic routes, carefully paced.

These examples are designed around sensible road travel. They can be shortened, extended or combined after discussing your flights, interests and preferred rhythm.

6–8 DAYS · FIRST INDIA JOURNEY

Essential Golden Triangle

Delhi · Agra · Jaipur · optional Abhaneri

Days 1–2

Delhi

Arrival, Old Delhi, Jama Masjid area, Chandni Chowk, Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar and selected New Delhi landmarks. Two nights are preferable after a long international flight.

Day 3

Delhi to Agra

Drive to Agra, then Agra Fort and a sunset viewpoint depending on arrival time. This avoids squeezing the Taj Mahal into a rushed transfer day.

Day 4

Agra to Jaipur

Taj Mahal early in the morning, followed by the road to Jaipur. Fatehpur Sikri or Chand Baori can be included as a meaningful stop, but not both if the day is to remain comfortable.

Days 5–6

Jaipur

Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal viewpoints, old-city markets and optional craft workshops. Add one extra night for a slower pace.

Day 7–8

Return or extension

Return to Delhi, fly onward, or continue to Pushkar, Ranthambore or Udaipur.

Why this route works

It limits hotel changes, allows two full city stays and avoids attempting Delhi, Agra and Jaipur in only four or five days.

Best for
First-time visitors, couples, families
Recommended pace
7 days; 8 after a late arrival
Main focus
Mughal and Rajput architecture, markets, urban culture
Possible addition
Ranthambore, Pushkar or Varanasi

9–11 DAYS · HERITAGE & WILDLIFE

Golden Triangle with Ranthambore

Delhi · Agra · Ranthambore · Jaipur

Days 1–2

Delhi

Arrival and a balanced introduction to Old and New Delhi.

Days 3–4

Agra

Agra Fort, Taj Mahal and sufficient time to avoid combining sightseeing with a very long onward journey.

Days 5–7

Ranthambore

Travel to Sawai Madhopur and allow two nights minimum. Safari permits are separate and sightings can never be guaranteed; the value lies in the forest experience as well as wildlife.

Days 8–10

Jaipur

Continue to Jaipur for forts, palaces, markets and a more relaxed end to the circuit.

Day 11

Delhi or onward flight

Return by road or connect according to the departure schedule.

Planning note

Ranthambore should not be treated as a quick roadside stop. Two nights provide a reasonable chance to schedule more than one safari and absorb delays.

Best for
Wildlife, photography, families with older children
Recommended pace
10 days
Important
Safari booking is subject to availability
Alternative
Bharatpur for birdlife

12–15 DAYS · CLASSIC RAJASTHAN

Rajasthan Discovery

Delhi · Jaipur · Pushkar · Jodhpur · Udaipur · optional Bundi

Days 1–2

Delhi

Arrival and the capital's main historical layers.

Days 3–5

Jaipur

Amber Fort, old city, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and time for craft traditions rather than only monument visits.

Day 6

Pushkar or Ajmer

A shorter transition day with a change of atmosphere before western Rajasthan.

Days 7–8

Jodhpur

Mehrangarh Fort, the Blue City lanes, markets and panoramic views.

Days 9–11

Udaipur via Ranakpur

Use the road day to visit Ranakpur, then allow two full nights for the City Palace, Lake Pichola and the old city.

Days 12–15

Bundi or return

Add Bundi for palaces, murals and stepwells, or return toward Jaipur/Delhi with an intermediate night if needed.

Why this route works

It avoids adding Jaisalmer merely to collect another city. Jaisalmer is rewarding, but its position requires additional days and should be planned as a genuine extension.

Best for
Architecture, markets, photography, food
Recommended pace
13–14 days
Optional extension
Jaisalmer +3 or 4 days
Slower alternative
Add Bundi and remove one major city

10–13 DAYS · ART, TEMPLES & THE GANGES

Spiritual and Cultural North India

Delhi · Agra · Gwalior · Orchha · Khajuraho · Varanasi

Days 1–2

Delhi

Arrival and selected heritage sites before heading south.

Days 3–4

Agra and Gwalior

Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, followed by Gwalior Fort as a useful bridge toward Madhya Pradesh.

Days 5–6

Orchha

Fort complex, riverside cenotaphs and time to experience a smaller historical town without rushing through it.

Days 7–8

Khajuraho

Western and Eastern temple groups, museum time and optional sound-and-light programme.

Days 9–12

Varanasi

Travel by train or flight connection where practical. Allow at least two nights for dawn on the Ganges, evening Aarti, Sarnath and the old lanes.

Transport strategy

This route combines road travel with rail or domestic flight segments. Amit can coordinate the road sectors, while the longest transfer to Varanasi is better evaluated separately.

Best for
Culture, temples, spirituality, history
Recommended pace
12–13 days
Important
Varanasi deserves at least two nights
Alternative
Delhi–Agra–Lucknow–Varanasi

16–19 DAYS · COMPREHENSIVE JOURNEY

Grand North India

Delhi · Agra · Jaipur · Pushkar · Jodhpur · Udaipur · Varanasi

Days 1–6

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur

A well-paced Golden Triangle forms the beginning of the journey.

Days 7–12

Pushkar, Jodhpur and Udaipur

Continue west through Rajasthan, including scenic and cultural stops rather than simply driving between hotels.

Days 13–15

Transition to Varanasi

Return toward a suitable airport or rail connection. A domestic flight is often more sensible than an excessive overland detour.

Days 16–18

Varanasi

Dawn boat ride, ghats, Sarnath, evening ceremonies and free time to absorb the city.

Day 19

Delhi connection

Fly back to Delhi according to the international departure schedule.

Planning principle

A grand itinerary should combine road and air intelligently. Covering every sector by car would add fatigue without adding meaningful travel value.

Best for
Travellers with two and a half weeks
Recommended pace
18–19 days
Main focus
India's major northern cultural contrasts
Could add
Ranthambore or Bundi, with extra days

7–12 DAYS · MOUNTAINS & LOCAL CULTURE

Himalayan Escape

Delhi · selected route through Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Kashmir or Ladakh

Days 1–2

Delhi and departure north

Arrive in Delhi and begin the mountain journey by road, train or domestic flight according to the chosen region and season.

Days 3–5

Hill towns and valleys

Explore mountain communities, local markets, temples or monasteries without compressing long road sectors into the same sightseeing day.

Days 6–8

Scenic roads and nature

Continue through valleys, forested landscapes and panoramic routes, with stops adapted to altitude, weather and road conditions.

Days 9–12

Deeper extension or return

Add another Himalayan region, remain longer in one valley, or return toward Delhi using the most practical road, rail or air connection.

Planning principle

There is no single Himalayan circuit. Amit builds the route around the season, available days, altitude tolerance and the traveller's interests.

Best for
Nature, mountains, photography, monasteries
Recommended pace
At least 7 days; 10–12 for a fuller journey
Important
Weather and road conditions can change the plan
Possible regions
Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Kashmir or Ladakh

A NOTE ON FEASIBILITY

More cities do not automatically create a better journey.

Road conditions, traffic, monument opening days, hotel changes, train schedules and domestic flights all affect the final route. Amit and Mario will help reduce unnecessary transfers and protect the time you actually spend experiencing each place.
